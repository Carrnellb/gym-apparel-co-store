# Gym Apparel CO. Online Store

Front-end website for **Gym Apparel CO. LLC**, an athletic brand that sells basketballs, compression socks, and logo caps directly to customers online. This repository holds the course project for SDC260 (Blueprints to Browsers).

**Author:** Carrnell Baskerville
**Started:** November 15, 2026

## Purpose

The site lets a shopper browse the product lines (Gym Ball, Gym Soxx, Gurl Soxx, and Logo Caps), read the details of a product, add items to a cart, and complete a checkout form. The goal is a clean, accessible storefront that puts the gym rat logo and the black, gold, and cream brand colors front and center.

## Planned Pages

| Page | File | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Store Homepage | `index.html` | Brand introduction, category links, email sign-up |
| Shop | `shop.html` | All products, grouped by category |
| Product Detail | `product.html` | Photos, description, size, price, Add to Cart |
| Cart | `cart.html` | Review items and order total |
| Checkout | `checkout.html` | Shipping and payment form |
| About / Contact | `about.html` | Brand story and contact form |
